# Golden Cosmetics — demo storefront

A standalone, Arabic RTL sample storefront following the Eva Store shopping experience.

- Live demo: https://g.eva-store.workers.dev
- Health check: https://g.eva-store.workers.dev/health
- Instagram reference: https://www.instagram.com/goldencosmetics_8/
- Telegram reference from the supplied profile: https://t.me/golden_gosmtks

## Included
- Responsive storefront for desktop and mobile.
- Category browsing, product search, product detail modal.
- Cart and favorites saved in the visitor's browser.
- Sample checkout that opens Telegram's share composer.

## Important
This is a design preview, not a live commerce backend. Product names, descriptions, images and IQD prices are illustrative placeholders until the owner confirms the actual catalogue, availability, prices, delivery terms and contact links. Orders are not stored on a server and the Telegram share action does not send automatically.
